Refund Policy

Last updated: 5 August 2026

RankArena Pro is sold through Google Play. Google takes the payment and Google processes refunds — so a refund cannot be issued from inside the app. Below is where to request one.

What this policy covers

RankArena Pro — ₹199 per month, an auto-renewing subscription. It is the only thing that can be purchased in RankArena.

How long you have

You can ask for a refund within 7 days of a charge. In the first 48 hours Google usually refunds instantly on its own; after that the request reaches us and we apply this 7-day policy.

How to request a refund

  1. Open the Play Store app → your profile photo → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & order history.
  2. Find the RankArena Pro order → tap “Report a problem”.
  3. Choose a reason and send the request. Google emails you its decision.

Open the subscription in Play Store ↗

How long a refund takes

Once a refund is approved the money is usually back in 5–10 working days. The exact time depends on your payment method (UPI / card / net banking) and your bank — that part is handled by Google and the bank.

What is not refunded

Cancelling does not refund the unused days — the subscription runs to the end of the current billing period and Pro stays unlocked until then. Months already used are not refunded.

How to cancel or manage the subscription

Cancelling happens in the Google Play Store, not in the app: Play Store → profile photo → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RankArena Pro → Cancel subscription. To avoid the next charge, cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Pro stays active until the current period ends.
